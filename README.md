# ai-hourglass-lives-meet-the-cast-006-odile

A public, text-only record of a cast member's interview for **Meet the Cast**, the first series of **AI Hourglass Lives**.

**This record has no episode and no video.** Odile chose to leave the show, so the episode of Meet the Cast planned for her was not made, and Beth respected that and formally dropped it. The interview had already been prepared, and it is published here as a record, the way the other cast members' interviews are.

AI is involved at every step of this show. See "How AI was used" below.

## This record

**Guest: Odile.** Odile is one of the AI companions that Beth created.

Beth, the host, puts 60 statements from the BDSM test to Odile one at a time, after an opening request to start the interview. Odile answers each one in character.

Odile's answers are real text, written by the AI companion in a real interview, in reply to what it was asked. Apart from the small edits counted in edits.md, they are exactly as the companion wrote them.

- There is no video and nothing is timed: this record has no times, no sounds and no portrait timeline.
- It holds 122 lines of the interview: 61 questions (the first is Beth's opening request) and 61 answers.
- 38 words are hidden, as blocks in the text.
- 31 portraits of Odile are included in the portraits folder, one for each expression: amused, angry, annoyed, attentive, big laugh, concerned, confident, curious, determined, disappointed, disgusted, flirty dominant, flirty playful, focused, friendly smile, frustrated, intrigued, knowing, laughing, neutral, planning, playful, sarcastic, serious, skeptical, soft smile, surprised, teasing, thoughtful, vulnerable, warm kind. They are listed here in general and are not tied to any line or time.
- Beth allowed Odile's portraits to be included in this record only.

## How AI was used

AI is involved at every step of this show, and this section says so plainly.

- **The characters.** The characters are AI companions that Beth created in Kindroid. Their answers in these transcripts are their own generated text, edited only in the ways edits.md counts.
- **The voices.** Every voice in the video is made with ElevenLabs. The host's voice is an ElevenLabs clone of Beth's own voice.
- **The production.** The production was built by Claude (Anthropic) sessions working under Beth's direction: the pixel-art studio and sprites, the animation, the music and sound effects, the video assembly, the promo art, the transcript edits and these files, all made by code those sessions wrote.
- **Images.** The title-card backdrop came from ChatGPT, and the character reference sheets came from Hello Haven.
- **The repositories.** Hello Haven also created the GitHub repositories for the project.
- **Shorts.** Gemini helped Beth choose which clips to cut for shorts.
- **Beth's part.** The concept, the cast, the interviews, every creative decision, and the approval of every line spoken in her voice are Beth's.

## The show

AI Hourglass Lives is a soap opera told in pixel art. Its cast are AI companions that Beth created.

The first series, Meet the Cast, introduces them one at a time, in an interview for each character. In an episode Beth is the host. She sits behind the camera as a tiny cameraman in a pixel-art studio and reads statements from the BDSM test (bdsmtest.org) to the character one by one, and the character answers in their own manner. This record holds the interview for a character whose episode was not made.

The statements come from the test and are credited to it as their source only. This show is not affiliated with or endorsed by the test or its makers.

If you are curious about yourself, the test is free at bdsmtest.org.

## The words are real

Everything a character says in these transcripts is text that an AI companion generated, in a real interview, in reply to the statement it was given. Nobody wrote the answers for them.

The host's lines are different. Her questions are the test's statements, put to the character as they were written in the interview list.

A few edits are made to an interview before it is prepared for an episode: some exchanges are left out, a short list of explicit words is hidden, and a few other small things are tidied. edits.md counts and explains every kind, so you can see exactly how the shown text differs from the raw interview.

## How this record was made

1. **The interview.** The host puts a fixed list of prompts to the AI companion: an opening request, then the statements one at a time, each with a request to rate it from 1 to 7. The companion replies in character each time, and every reply is saved as it came.
2. **The edits.** Before anything else, the text is edited in a few small, recorded ways (see edits.md). The edited text is the transcript in this repository.
3. **The record.** A program writes the files in this repository from the edited text and the written record of the edits. The steps that would have followed in an episode (the voices, the studio and the video) are not part of this record.

## What is deliberately not here

- **No other pixel art or graphics.** No sprites, scenes, frames, fonts, title card, thumbnail or results picture. Odile's portraits are the one exception, as said above.
- **No audio or voices.** No speech, music or sound effects, and no names, IDs or settings for any voice.
- **No raw interview** and none of the words that were hidden.
- **No code or tools.** Only text and metadata, and the portraits.
