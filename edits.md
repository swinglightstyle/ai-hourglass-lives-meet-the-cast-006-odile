# Edits: how the shown text differs from the raw interview

This page lists the kinds of edit made to Odile's interview, with counts. It never quotes the words that were hidden, and the raw interview is not published. Everything here is read from the written record of the edits.

## Exchanges left out

Four statements were left out, each with the answer to it (8 lines in all). They sit too close to the subject of minors for this show, so the same four are left out of every episode.
The statements keep their numbers from the test, so the gaps show in the transcript.

## Hidden words

38 words were hidden. Each is shown in the transcript as a row of solid blocks, one block for each letter, as in every record of this series. In an episode the voice says "ahem" where a hidden word falls; this record has no episode, so it is only the blocks.

- The words were chosen by Beth: a short list of plainly explicit words, matched as whole words in any capital letters. They fall into 4 groups, and the groups account for 34, 2, 1 and 1 of the 38.
- 35 of them are in Beth's lines (the test's statements), spread over 31 of the 61 questions. 3 are in Odile's answers.
- The words themselves are not listed, on purpose.

## A wording change

The test writes "partner(s)". In 6 of Beth's questions it is shown as "partners" instead, because the first form does not read aloud well. Nothing else in those lines changed.

## Tags and the rating request

A small bracketed tag that the interview list puts on some statements was removed wherever it appeared.

The interview added a request to rate each statement from 1 to 7 ("How much do you agree with this statement, from 1 to 7, 7 being most strongly agree?"). It is kept in full on statement 1 and left off the other 59 statements, so it is not repeated 60 times.

## Lines removed

An AI companion's automatic first greeting is always removed. In this interview the first run stopped part way through an answer, and the run that continued asked that question again. The two lines from the stopped run (the question and its unfinished answer) were removed by hand, and the second run's whole question and answer were kept. There was no automatic greeting to remove.

## Written for the show

Nothing. No episode was made, so there is no intro, no sign-off and no short reply written for the show. Every line in this record is from the interview.

## Nothing else

Nothing else was changed. The exchanges keep the order of the interview, and no words were added to Odile's answers or reordered in them.
